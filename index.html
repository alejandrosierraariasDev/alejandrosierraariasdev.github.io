About

Programs that land on time and hold together afterwards

I run the day-to-day of delivery (stakeholder reporting, DORA metrics, budgets and SLAs) while owning the Quality Engineering strategy for the programs I run. That QE experience shapes how I manage delivery: I know what good testing costs, what it catches, and how to plan around it instead of after it.

I build the AI-native tooling that keeps quality moving at the pace of delivery, and represent technical strategy in Sngular's CTO Office and in client-facing RFP work.

People come first. Always. A good leader helps, detects problems before they explode, prevents what can be prevented, and protects the team and the company at the same time, anticipating what the stakeholder will need instead of waiting to be asked.

I plan first and decide on evidence: executing a plan is hard, and working without one is gambling. That's why I obsess over process, own the outcome, and step up when a crisis needs someone to take the wheel.

Spotting where someone can grow and helping them get there is the hardest and most satisfying part of being a manager. I also ask to be challenged back, through anonymous surveys, retros and open conversations, because that's the only way I really know what to improve.
