## The problem

In many teams, test automation lives apart from the tools where work is planned. Test cases are written by hand from user stories, failures are triaged manually, and proving what was tested for a release, or for an audit, takes days.

## How it works

- **Jira + Xray** are the single source of truth for requirements, tests and defects.
- **Rovo**, Atlassian's AI, and **Xray AI** turn user stories into BDD test cases.
- **Claude**, connected through **MCP**, orchestrates the flow across Jira, Xray and Playwright.
- **Playwright** runs in **Bitbucket Pipelines**, with every result traced back to Xray.
- **Every failure becomes a Jira defect automatically**, with dashboards for developers, QA managers and the business.

## The result

- A continuous, auditable flow from user story to test result to defect.
- Less manual glue between tools, and faster triage.
- Quality you can audit, at delivery speed, without adding headcount.
