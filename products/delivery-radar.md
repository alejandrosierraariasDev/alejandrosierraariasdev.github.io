## The problem

Delivery health is usually judged by opinion: status meetings, traffic-light reports and whoever speaks loudest. Every team is measured differently, so problems surface late and comparisons are impossible.

## How it works

Delivery Radar connects to the tools a team already uses: **Jira** and **GitLab, GitHub or Bitbucket**, including their CI.

- It applies **23 rules across 8 dimensions**: process, requirements, flow, delivery, quality, CI, traceability and risk.
- Rules go from pipelines where no check can actually break the build to growing bug debt.
- Every finding is **prioritized and comes with traceable evidence**, not a bare percentage.
- A new client is **a configuration file, not new code**, and each one builds its own historical trend.

## The result

- Any team can be audited in the same way, from facts rather than opinions.
- Leaders get a short, prioritized list of what to fix first, with the evidence to back it.
- Trends show whether changes actually worked.

Delivery Radar grew out of the four-pillar measurement system I built (Impact, Efficiency, Quality, Velocity), which tracks DORA metrics automatically across 52 Jira projects and 65+ repositories with zero manual reporting.
